This Privacy Policy applies to the Pasea mobile application (package name: com.thegentleman.pasea), developed by The Gentleman Developer (hereinafter referred to as the "App"). The App functions strictly as an offline/local password generation tool designed to help users generate random passwords based on selected criteria (such as numbers, symbols, and letters) and copy them to their device's clipboard.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE
We believe in total privacy. The App DOES NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive user data, or device information.

- No Account Required: You can use all features of the App without creating an account or logging in.
